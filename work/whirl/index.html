Work / Whirl

Choosing where to eat is a chore, so we made it a quick round of cards.

Whirl is a concept for an AI assistant that helps people find what to do and where to eat in their city, and helps new restaurants reach them.

The challenge

A coupon partner program wanted to close the gap between new restaurants and the people living around them.

The idea was to connect the two through discounts. Whirl was created as a concept for a company case study, to show how AI could improve that customer journey through conversational patterns.

Our approach

Instead of asking people to set filters, Whirl lets them play their way to a decision.

  1. Sort, don't search

    A simple card sorting model helps people choose where to eat. They eliminate the places they don't want, and the app learns their preferences from what is left.

  2. Ask in plain language

    An AI assistant answers personal requests with live information, such as dietary restrictions or pet-friendly places, and replaces filters altogether.

  3. Give owners a voice

    Short videos of places to go and things to see let restaurant owners upload their own content and show what makes them worth a visit.

  4. Build the identity

    The Whirl logo was handwritten to look like it is spiralling, then carried into an app icon that sits naturally in the App Store and on the home screen.

The solution

The assistant suggests a place that matches your preferences, then offers a useful next step, like seeing the menu before you go.

  • A discovery home with nearby places and offers for newcomers
  • Card sorting that narrows the choice in a few swipes
  • A chat that handles requests filters can't, from pet-friendly tables to parking
